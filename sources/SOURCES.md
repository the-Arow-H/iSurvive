# Sources

Every field-guide page names one or more of these ids in its frontmatter.
United States government works are public domain. Other items are linked,
not copied. Stop the Bleed and Red Cross course materials are trademarks;
this guide links to them and does not reproduce their curricula.

The FEMA US&R appendix below is public domain and is used for shelter
priority and the debris hut. This guide does **not** use that appendix's
20-minute boil, its "all cactus is safe" line, or its solar-still yield.
Those conflict with current CDC water guidance or are broader than this
guide will repeat.
