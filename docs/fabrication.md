# Printed modules (P)

These are repairable polymer parts. Kit buyers receive them. Self-source prints them.

Until STEP/STL land in `kits/print/`, use the tray/bay as a **fit envelope**, not a vanity print.

| PN | Fits | Envelope (mm, estimate) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CM-TRAY | SBC + storage + USB-C hub | 130 × 90 × 35 | Vent the SoC. M3 inserts at corners. |
| PM-BAY | 65W bank + folded solar sleeve | 180 × 90 × 45 | Strap slots, not glue. |
| WT-MOUNT | Hollow-fiber on 1L bottle | 80 × 40 × 25 | Keep the cartridge off the case floor. |
| SH-POUCH | Stakes, cord, ferro | 120 × 80 × 20 | Drain holes. |
| SPARES | Latch + tray patch | set | One extra latch, one flat patch. |
| ISV-MN-01 | Field manual | A5 booklet | Print from `operator/knowledge/`. |

Do not substitute random PETG for a load path without a reprint test. Fasteners are M3/M4 from ISV-RP-01.
