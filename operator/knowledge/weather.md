In {{climate}} {{setting}}, the weather decides the next hour more than the map does.

For {{people}} person(s) and about {{hours}} hours:

- Wet: fly first, then floor. Keep the SBC and filter out of splash.
- Cold: insulation under you, bank inside a layer, wet filter off the snow.
- Hot: shade the bank and the board. Drink on a schedule.
- Wind: site below the ridge. Stake the bivy or it becomes a sail.
- Urban: interior rooms, flood-aware. Do not shelter in a closed garage with a generator.

Reassess at half the remaining hours, not at panic.
