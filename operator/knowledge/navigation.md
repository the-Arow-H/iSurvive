Download maps to the phone before you need them. The operator does not replace a compass.

For {{people}} person(s) and about {{hours}} hours in {{setting}}:

- Mark two outs: the way you came, and one alternate that does not share a choke point.
- Walk timed legs. If the weather is {{climate}}, reassess at half the remaining hours, not at dusk panic.
- Urban: stay off flooded underpasses; use high corridors; do not treat a dead phone as a reason to split the group.
- Wild: ridgelines for signal, valleys for wind and water. Do not follow a drainage just because it is downhill.
- Comms: a written rally point beats a hope that the mesh comes back.
