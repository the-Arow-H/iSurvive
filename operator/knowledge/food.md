This kit is not a pantry. For {{people}} person(s) and about {{hours}} hours in {{climate}} {{setting}}:

- Water is the constraint. Do not add salt-heavy food until the filter is producing.
- Under 24 h: finish what you already carry. Do not spend daylight foraging.
- Multi-day: simple calories you can eat cold. Fire is for water and warmth first.
- Urban: sealed commercial food over anything from a flood line or a warm cooler.

No medical nutrition advice. If someone cannot keep water down, that is emergency services, not a kit problem.
