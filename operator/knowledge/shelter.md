In {{setting}} under {{climate}} conditions, spend the first minutes on a dry, wind-aware pad — not on a pretty view.

For {{people}} person(s) and about {{hours}} hours:

- Site: high enough to shed water, low enough to stay out of ridge wind. Avoid dry creek beds.
- Bivy is a vapor barrier. Vent it or you will soak your own insulation.
- Headlamp on red at night. Spare cells live in the printed pouch, not loose in the case.
- Ferro rod throws sparks onto prepared tinder. Build a small, contained fire only where it is legal and you can drown it.
- If {{hours}} is under 12, a windbreak and dry ground beat a fancy camp.

Wet: fly or tarp first, then floor. Keep the SBC and filter out of splash.
Cold: insulation under you matters more than over you.
Hot: shade and airflow. Do not wrap in the bivy in high heat unless you need a vapor barrier against rain.
Urban: interior rooms without exterior glass, away from flood lines.
