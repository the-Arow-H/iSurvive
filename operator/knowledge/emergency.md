iSurvive does not treat injury or illness.

If someone is hurt, unresponsive, or you are in immediate danger: contact local emergency services. Do not put medical details into this repo, the operator notes field, or an X draft.

What the kit can still do while you wait for trained help:

- Keep the scene from getting worse: traffic, weather, fire.
- Water, shelter, light, power for the phone that places the call.
- Written location if you have a signal plan.

Get certified first-aid training on a calendar, not from a language model.
