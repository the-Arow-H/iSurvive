Printed parts are meant to crack and be replaced. Fasteners are meant to be reused.

For {{people}} person(s) in {{setting}}:

- Inventory the M3/M4 assortment before dark. A missing nut is a future failure.
- Swap a cracked tray panel from the spare set. Do not glue structural prints as a first repair — reprint or bolt a patch.
- Process codes: P = polymer print, X = off-the-shelf. Do not substitute a printed latch with random hardware that will shear.
- Cordage and tape buy time. They are not the permanent fix.
- After a drop, check the SBC seating, filter housing, and solar hinge before you walk on.

If you are on the self-source path, fabricate to the part number on the BOM, not to a screenshot.
