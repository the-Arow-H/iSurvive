Asimov 1 is the pattern, not the promise: kit **or** self-source, printed repairable modules, dual compute, own the stack. This is not a humanoid.

Assembly order for ISV-FK-01:

1. Compute tray and SBC. Confirm storage is seated.
2. Power bay, bank, solar. One full charge cycle on the bench.
3. Water module leak-check with clean tap water — not with a field source.
4. Shelter pouch packed last so it is first out.
5. Repair module on top of the spare panels, not buried.

Self-source: buy X-coded lines from the BOM, print P-coded lines, skip kit-only freight and QC lines. Your landed cost is yours; the public SKU price still obeys `price >= landed / 0.70` for sold kits.

Do not invert the project build order: kit sales first, then dual host, then the free Field Kit Operator template, then build-in-public drafts.
