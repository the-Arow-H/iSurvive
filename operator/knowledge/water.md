Treat every untreated source in {{setting}} weather ({{climate}}) as unsafe until it has gone through the hollow-fiber filter.

For {{people}} person(s) and about {{hours}} hours:

- Count liters, not bottles. Plan 3 L per person per 24 h as a working floor; raise it in heat.
- Filter from the clearest moving water you can reach. Rest the bag. Pre-filter grit through a cloth.
- If the water is cloudy, let it settle, then filter. Do not force a clogged cartridge.
- Backup tabs are for when the filter is frozen, smashed, or the water is chemically suspect. Read the tab label. They are not a daily substitute.
- Keep the printed mount on the bottle so the filter is not rolling in the case mud.

Cold: sleep with the wet filter in an inner layer so it does not freeze solid.
Hot / desert: drink on a schedule even if you are not thirsty; shade the bottle.
Urban: prefer closed building tanks and sealed supplies over decorative fountains and flood water.
