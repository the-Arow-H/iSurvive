If you need rescue, make a shape someone else already knows. For {{people}} person(s) in {{setting}}:

- Stay put once you have water, shelter, and a signal plan — unless the site is unsafe.
- Contrast: open ground, three of anything, light vs dark.
- Headlamp at night on a schedule, not a strobe that kills the cells.
- Urban: a lit window or a marked door beats running a grid.

This is not a substitute for contacting local emergency services when you can.
