This is a stub. The plains are open, so the Park Service warning about lightning in the middle of a field and on high points is the site rule. Do not be the tallest object. The FEMA appendix says to put shelter on the lee side of the weather and out of washes and depressions. A dry creek in grassland is still a flood path.

Wind is the shelter problem. A low lean-to or a debris hut in a coulee that is not a flood channel beats a tall tent on a ridge. Ground insulation still matters. The plains get cold enough for hypothermia when you are wet. That care page is a clinical draft.

Water is often scarce and still needs treatment when you find it. Stock tanks and ditches can hold chemicals or algae. CDC says you cannot boil fuel or toxins out of water. If it smells like fuel or a chemical, leave it.

Dandelion is documented across the United States, including the plains states on the FEIS list. Cattail is a wetland plant, not a prairie-wide one. Use the biome filter. Never eat on a single identification.

Trapping and hunting follow the state. The legality page does not change because the land looks empty.
