**NOT MEDICAL ADVICE.** Setting a bone is a clinician's job. MedlinePlus says not to straighten a bone or test whether it still moves. If you think the head, neck, or back is hurt, do not move the person except to get them out of immediate danger, and do not try to reposition a suspected spine injury.

MedlinePlus broken-bone first aid, paraphrased:

- Check airway and breathing. Call emergency services and start CPR or bleeding control only if you are trained and it is needed.
- Keep the person still and calm. Look for other injuries.
- If help is coming soon, let them take the next steps.
- If skin is broken, call for emergency help. Do not breathe on the wound or probe it. Cover it with a sterile dressing if you have one. Do not try to line the bone up unless you are medically trained to do so.
- If you must immobilize, use a splint or sling. A rolled newspaper or strips of wood are the examples MedlinePlus gives. Immobilize above and below the injured bone.
- Do not move a person with an injured hip, pelvis, or upper leg unless you have to. If you must, MedlinePlus says to pull them to safety by their clothes.

## Splint

MedlinePlus "how to make a splint":

- Care for an open wound before the splint. Rinse and cover it.
- Splint in the position you found, unless a specialist has already treated that part.
- Use something rigid: sticks, boards, rolled newspaper. A rolled blanket or clothing if that is all you have. You can tape an injured finger to the next finger.
- Extend the splint past the injury. Include the joint above and the joint below when you can.
- Tie with cloth, a belt, or tape above and below the injury. Knots should not sit on the injury. Do not tie tight enough to cut off circulation.
- Pad the splint. Recheck for swelling, paleness, or numbness, and loosen it if those show up.
- If the pain is worse after the splint, take the splint off and get medical help.

MedlinePlus also says that if you are remote, no help is available, the part looks abnormally bent, and the limb is pale, gently placing it back toward a normal position may improve circulation. That sentence is an exception on their page, not the default. Do not treat it as permission to haul on a joint. This draft flags it for the clinical reviewer. When help exists, do not realign.

## Sling

For an arm, MedlinePlus says to splint first if a splint is needed, then sling. A triangular cloth works: elbow at the top point, wrist along the base, free ends tied at the neck, hand higher than the elbow, elbow near a right angle. Fingertips stay visible so you can see color. Remove watches and rings. Tie the arm to the chest if it must stay still. Loosen anything that makes the hand cool, pale, blue, numb, or tingling.

Get help for bone through the skin, an open wound, loss of feeling, loss of a pulse or of warmth, or fingers and toes that turn blue and numb. Those are MedlinePlus reasons to seek care right away.
