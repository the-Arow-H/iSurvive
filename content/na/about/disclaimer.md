**Not medical advice. Not legal advice. Not a rescue service.**

The long form is DISCLAIMER.md in the repository. The short form is this:

If someone is badly hurt, confused, not breathing normally, or getting worse, contact local emergency services. Poison Control in the United States is 1-800-222-1222.

First-aid pages are drafts. Each one has `review: pending-clinical` until a licensed clinician, the pharmacist who owns this project, signs off. They cite CDC, Ready.gov, NPS, NIH MedlinePlus, and public Red Cross pages, and they link to Stop the Bleed. They do not give drug doses.

Never eat a wild plant on a single identification. This guide does not cover mushrooms. Poison hemlock, water hemlock, death camas, and yew are plants to avoid.

Hunting, fishing, and trapping rules are local. A page in this guide does not make a method legal.

Boil untreated water 1 minute, or 3 minutes above 6,500 feet, which is the CDC rule used here. Older pamphlets that say something else were not followed when they conflicted.

The guide is a static site. After the first load it is meant to work with the network off. It does not phone home.
